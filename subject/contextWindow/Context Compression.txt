Many advanced AI systems perform context compression automatically by:

* removing repetitive confirmations,
* removing greetings,
* removing duplicate information,
* keeping facts and decisions,
* keeping unresolved tasks.

This reduces token usage without losing as much information as summarization.

Differnce :
Summarization

Context Compression

Rewrites the conversation

Keeps the original conversation

Generates new text

Removes unnecessary text

Can lose details

Usually preserves more detail
