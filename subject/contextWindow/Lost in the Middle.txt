 Lost in the Middle 

                    Research has shown that models generally pay the most attention to:

* the beginning of the context,
* the end of the context,

and are more likely to miss important information buried in the middle.




System Prompt

Document 4

Document 5

Current User Question




If the correct answer is in Doc C, the model may miss it more often than if it were placed near the top of the retrieved context.



Production solution

* Rank retrieved documents by relevance.
* Put the most relevant documents first.
* Remove irrelevant documents.
* Don’t stuff the entire vector database into the prompt.
